Write-up

Design rationale

After analyzing the data and cleaning the data along with completing exploratory analysis on the data, we found that having a visualization documenting change over time would be the most effective method in answering the question. Where does warming concentrate by 2100, and how much of it does our emissions path decide? In doing so we wanted to show both temperature and precipitation over time along with predictions based on different emission paths. In deciding the color palette, we chose to use a red blue scale for temperature and a brown to blue-green scale for precipitation. This was done so as temperature often utilizes blue for colder measurements with red being hotter measurements. For the precipitation, we chose these contrasting colors to signify blue-green relating closely to rainfall and water with brown being a barren desert.

Development process

The work was split evenly between the three of us during each step of the development process with everyone contributing equally in exploratory data analysis along with the development of the website. It took roughly 15 hours to complete in total between the three of us in developing the visualization. The aspect that definitely took the longest time was implementing the interactive feature on the visualization as it took quite some time to make sure that it was working properly.
